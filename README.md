## Publications on Zenodo

### 1. Mars Rubber-Glass House Project – Design, Variants, Cost, and Vision for Martian Habitats

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.16493055.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16493055)

Technical design and conceptual framework for a 1000 m² hybrid habitat optimized for Mars, using silicone rubber, borosilicate glass, and titanium/Inconel structural elements. Includes detailed design documents, variant comparisons, and cost estimations for Earth-based testing and Mars deployment.

- **Published:** June 1, 2024 | **Version:** v1
- **Files:** 6 PDFs (DE/EN)
- **License:** CC BY 4.0 / MIT (mixed)

### 2. Three-Step Evolution Towards Biomimetic and Self-Growing Structures

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.16540444.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16540444)

A three-step conceptual evolution of Martian habitat construction, progressing from traditional engineering to biomimetic lightweight materials and ultimately to autonomous, self-growing silicon-based structures. Builds upon the Mars Rubber-Glass House Project.

- **Published:** September 13, 2024 | **Version:** 1.0
- **Community:** [Interplanetary Lab](https://zenodo.org/communities/imterplanetarylab/)
- **License:** CC BY 4.0

### 3. Mars Field Laboratories: Integrating Autonomous Legged Robots and Cooperative Robotic Clusters

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.15455156.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15455156)

An integrated exploration architecture combining autonomous legged robots (Type A missions) and cooperative robotic clusters (Type B missions) for Mars surface operations. Embeds social behaviors into robotic systems for high operational resilience.

- **Published:** April 29, 2025 | **Version:** 1.2
- **License:** CC BY 4.0
