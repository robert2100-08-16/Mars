# The Mars Book

**Collected Papers, Concepts and Engineering Studies on Mars Habitats, Biomimetic Structures and Autonomous Robotics**

---

*Robert Alexander Massinger*
Robert Alexander Massinger Space Technologies
Munich, Germany

---

Version 1.0.0 · February 2026

---

> *"Mars is there, waiting to be reached."*
> — Buzz Aldrin

---

This book brings together the author's published papers, design concepts and
engineering studies developed between 2024 and 2026. The work spans three
domains: inflatable rubber-glass habitats that can shelter early settlers,
biomimetic materials that may one day grow themselves, and autonomous robots
that prepare the terrain before humans arrive.

Every chapter can be read independently. Where a chapter has been published as
a standalone paper on Zenodo, its DOI is printed in the chapter header.
