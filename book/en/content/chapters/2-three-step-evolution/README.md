# 2. Three-Step Evolution

> Biomimetic and Self-Growing Structures: A Vision for Adaptive Habitats on Mars

**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.16540444](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16540444)

---

## Overview

This chapter presents a three-step conceptual evolution of Martian habitat construction,
progressing from traditional engineering principles to biomimetic lightweight materials
and ultimately to autonomous, self-growing silicon-based structures. Building upon the
foundational Mars Rubber-Glass House Project, this work outlines a scalable, sustainable
pathway for future Mars habitats.
