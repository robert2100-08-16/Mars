## Abstract

Future Mars missions require robotic systems that can transcend the limitations of
wheeled rovers. "Mars Field Laboratories" proposes an integrated exploration architecture
combining autonomous legged robots (Type A missions) and cooperative robotic clusters
(Type B missions). Individual robots perform tactical sorties equipped with mobile
laboratories for initial in-situ analyses, while clusters enable strategic operations
with shared infrastructure and advanced laboratory modules.

By embedding social behaviors into the robotic systems — such as consensus-based health
monitoring, decentralized swarm coordination, and peer-assisted recovery — this approach
achieves high operational resilience and scientific throughput. The concept aims to form
a miniature, self-sustaining robotic society on Mars, significantly enhancing exploration
capabilities.
