# 3. Mars Field Laboratories

> Autonomous Legged Robots and Cooperative Robotic Clusters for Mars Exploration

**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.15455156](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15455156)

---

## Overview

Future Mars missions require robotic systems that can transcend the limitations of
wheeled rovers. "Mars Field Laboratories" proposes an integrated exploration architecture
combining autonomous legged robots (Type A missions) and cooperative robotic clusters
(Type B missions). By embedding social behaviors into the robotic systems, this approach
achieves high operational resilience and scientific throughput.
