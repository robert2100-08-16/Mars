## Abstract

The Mars Rubber-Glass House Project presents the technical design and conceptual
framework for a 1000 m² hybrid habitat optimized for Mars. Using silicone rubber,
borosilicate glass, and titanium/Inconel structural elements, the project explores
a resilient and scalable building solution for early Martian colonization. The
publication includes detailed design documents, variant comparisons, and cost
estimations for Earth-based testing and Mars deployment. Special attention is given
to sustainable resource usage, construction logistics, and the feasibility of local
material production on Mars.
